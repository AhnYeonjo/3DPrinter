⚡ BED-OUT Pin Configuration — Important difference from earlier documentation!
In the official pinout, BED-OUT consists of VIN (constant power supplied from BED-POWER) and PF5 (MOSFET low-side switched GND).
That is, when PF5 is ON, it connects to GND, allowing current to flow.
SSR Connection: BED-OUT VIN → SSR DC(+) / BED-OUT PF5 → SSR DC(-)

⚡ How BED-OUT Works (Low-Side MOSFET)
PF5 on BED-OUT is a Low-Side MOSFET output. When Klipper drives PF5 HIGH, the MOSFET turns ON -> PF5 connects to GND -> current flows through the SSR DC input -> SSR AC output conducts -> heater pad warms up.
When PF5 is LOW, the MOSFET turns OFF -> circuit opens -> heating stops.
BED-POWER must be connected for voltage to be present on BED-OUT VIN!

🔧 Step-by-Step Wiring Procedure (Kraken V1.0 Official Pinout)
🔴 Pre-Work Safety Check
Unplug the AC power cord or switch off the breaker before starting. Verify 0V with a multimeter.
  1. Wago 221 AC Mains Distribution
    Distribute L, N, and PE from the AC inlet into three 5-port Wago 221 blocks.
    L Rail: AC Inlet L -> 48V PSU, 24V PSU, 5V PSU, SSR (via 10A fuse)
    N Rail: AC Inlet N -> 48V PSU, 24V PSU, 5V PSU, Heater Pad N (White wire)
    PE Rail: AC Inlet PE -> 48V PSU, 24V PSU, 5V PSU, Bed Frame Earth (Blue wire)
  2. PSU DC Outputs -> Kraken Power Inputs
    48V PSU → Kraken MOTOR-POWER (VIN + GND + HV)
    24V PSU → Kraken POWER (VIN + GND) AND BED-POWER (VIN + GND)
    Note: Connect both POWER and BED-POWER to the 24V PSU. BED-OUT will not function if BED-POWER is unpowered!
  3. Mount SSR Heatsink & Secure to DIN Rail
    Apply thermal compound and mount the SSR onto a DIN-rail heatsink. Secure to the DIN rail in a well-ventilated electronics bay area.
  4. AC Load Wiring: Wago L Rail -> Fuse -> SSR -> Thermal Fuse -> Heater Pad
    Wago L Rail 4th port -> 10A Fuse -> SSR ① L-IN
    SSR ② L-OUT -> Thermal Fuse (125C) -> Heater Pad L (Thick White Wire 1)
    Heater Pad N (Thick White Wire 2) -> Wago N Rail
    Secure the thermal fuse flush against the heater pad with Kapton tape (if not already integrated inside the Keenovo pad).
  5. Protective Earth (PE) Grounding
    Heater pad blue wire + ring terminal -> bolt to aluminum bed frame -> connect to Wago PE Rail
    Note: Bolt the factory ring terminal directly to the bed frame, and route the other end to the Wago PE Rail.
  6. DC Control Wiring: Kraken BED-OUT -> SSR
    Kraken BED-OUT VIN → SSR ③ DC+
    Kraken BED-OUT PF5 → SSR ④ DC-
    Wire Gauge: 22–24AWG (low-current control signal). Verify polarity!
    VIN = Constant 24V (+), PF5 = Pulled to GND (-) when MOSFET is ON
  7. Thermistor Wiring: Heater Pad -> Kraken THB
    Route the two thin red wires from the center patch -> Kraken THB port (PB0 + GND)
    Non-polarized. Route the red thermistor wires away from the white AC mains wires to prevent EMI noise.
  8. Pre-Power Inspection & Functional Test
    1. Verify infinite resistance (isolation) between AC mains and DC logic rails with a multimeter
    2. Verify thermistor resistance (~100k ohm at 25C room temperature)
    3. Confirm 24V is connected to BED-POWER
    4. Power on and confirm Klipper reads ambient room temperature (~20–30C) on heater_bed
    5. Test heating with SET_HEATER_TEMPERATURE HEATER=heater_bed TARGET=50

💡 Mandatory PID Calibration
After assembly, run PID_CALIBRATE HEATER=heater_bed TARGET=110 -> SAVE_CONFIG
High-wattage AC beds heat rapidly and require proper PID tuning to prevent overshoot.
✅ Post-Wiring Safety Checklist
  • ●AC / DC Isolation — Verify infinite resistance between AC mains and DC logic wiring
  • ●PE Earth Continuity — Verify < 1 ohm between Aluminum Bed Frame, Wago PE Rail, and AC Inlet PE
  • ●SSR Polarity — Confirm BED-OUT VIN -> SSR ③DC+ and BED-OUT PF5 -> SSR ④DC-
  • ●BED-POWER Feed — Confirm 24V PSU is wired to BED-POWER (VIN+GND)
  • ●Thermal Fuse — Verify 125C thermal fuse is mounted in firm contact with the heater pad
  • ●Thermistor Separation — Keep thin red thermistor wires separated by >= 10mm from AC mains lines
  • ●Wago Termination — Verify strip length and confirm all Wago 221 levers are fully latched
  • ●Ambient Reading — Confirm THB (PB0) reports ~20–30C in Klipper before heating
  • ●Heating Test — Set TARGET=50 and confirm smooth temperature rise and regulation
  • ●PID Tuning — Run PID_CALIBRATE TARGET=110 -> SAVE_CONFIG
  • ●SSR Heatsink Temp — Confirm SSR heatsink stays below 80C during 110C bed soak
